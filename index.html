Privacy Policy for SoleStore

Last updated: August 2026

SoleStore ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SoleStore mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order history and transaction details

How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order status updates
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Improve our app and services

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. All data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as needed to fulfill your orders.

Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your data at any time through the Profile screen in the app. Use the "Delete My Account" option to permanently remove all your data.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy: admin@solestore.pk
